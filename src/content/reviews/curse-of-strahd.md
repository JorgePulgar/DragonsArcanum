## The Dark Prince of Barovia

Curse of Strahd is widely considered the best adventure ever produced for D&D 5e. It transports players to the demiplane of Barovia, a land trapped in eternal mists and ruled by the vampire count Strahd von Zarovich. The atmosphere is thick with dread, and every choice feels heavy with consequence.

![Castle Ravenloft](/images/strahd-cover.jpg)

### The Tarokka Deck

One of the most unique features of this adventure is the Tarokka reading. At the start of the campaign, the DM (or an NPC) performs a card reading that determines the location of several key items and the identity of the party's ally. This adds a layer of randomization that makes every playthrough feel unique.

## A Challenge for All

Barovia is a sandbox, but one where the edges are sharp. If the players wander into the wrong area too early—such as Old Bonegrinder at level 3—they are likely to face a TPK. This creates a sense of tension and fear that is perfectly suited for the gothic horror genre.

### Safety and Content

It is important to note that Curse of Strahd deals with very dark themes, including kidnapping, abuse, and suicide. We strongly recommend using safety tools like Lines and Veils or the X-Card when running this adventure to ensure everyone at the table is comfortable.

## Conclusion

If you want a campaign that your players will talk about for years, Curse of Strahd is the one. It requires a lot of prep from the DM to manage the complex NPCs and the sandbox nature of the valley, but the payoff is unmatched.
