## Return to Krynn

For the first time in 5th Edition, we return to the war-torn world of Krynn. *Shadow of the Dragon Queen* serves as both an adventure and a setting guide, introducing players to the unique flavors of Dragonlance—Knights of Solamnia, Mages of High Sorcery, and the kender.

![Dragon Army Invasion](/images/dragonlance-cover.jpg)

### The War of the Lance

The campaign takes place during the legendary War of the Lance. Unlike typical D&D adventures where heroes wander freely, here you are soldiers in a massive conflict. The backdrop of war is constant; cities burn, refugees flee, and Dragon Armies march. The atmosphere is top-notch.

## Mechanics of War

The book attempts to introduce a system for mass combat using the companion board game *Warriors of Krynn*. While an interesting experiment, in practice, switching between a TTRPG and a board game breaks immersion for many tables. Fortunately, the book provides tools to run these battles as "narrative encounters" instead.

### Character Options

The new backgrounds and feats are the highlight mechanically. Being a **Knight of Solamnia** or a **Mage of High Sorcery** gives you free feats at level 1 and 4, making your character feel significantly more powerful than a standard adventurer. This power creep is intentional, reflecting the epic nature of the setting.

## Final Verdict

If you want a tightly paced, combat-heavy campaign with a clear villain and high stakes, *Shadow of the Dragon Queen* is fantastic. Just be prepared to railroad your players a bit—this is a war story, not a sandbox.
