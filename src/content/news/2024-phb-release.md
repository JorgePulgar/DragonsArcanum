## Mark Your Calendars

It is official: the revised *Player's Handbook* for Dungeons & Dragons will hit shelves on **September 17, 2024**. This release kicks off the massive 50th-anniversary celebration of the world's greatest roleplaying game.

![New PHB Art](/images/phb-2024.jpg)

### Staggered Core Rulebooks

Unlike 5th Edition's initial release, the three core books will not drop simultaneously.
- **Player's Handbook**: September 17, 2024
- **Dungeon Master's Guide**: November 12, 2024
- **Monster Manual**: February 18, 2025

### Early Access

Pre-ordering on D&D Beyond will grant early digital access starting September 3rd. Local game stores will also have copies available early, continuing WotC's support for brick-and-mortar retailers.

## What's Changing?

This is not a new edition (don't call it 6e!), but a "revision." Expect massive overhauls to the Ranger and Monk classes, the new Weapon Mastery system, and a completely reorganized layout designed to make the game easier to learn than ever before.
