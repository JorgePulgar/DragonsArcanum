## The Hype for 2024

As we approach the 50th anniversary of Dungeons & Dragons, the community is buzzing with anticipation for the "One D&D" initiative. While Wizards of the Coast has been tight-lipped about exact dates, several retail leaks and distributor schedules suggest a staggered release starting in late summer.

![D&D 50th Anniversary Logo](/images/one-dnd-news.jpg)

### What to Expect in the New Player's Handbook

The first book expected to drop is the revised *Player's Handbook*. Playtest data has shown significant changes to classes like the Fighter and Druid, and fans are eager to see the final versions. Rumors suggest a late August release, coinciding with major summer conventions.

## Impact on Existing Campaigns

One of the biggest questions is how these new rules will affect current 5e campaigns. WotC has maintained that the new content is "backwards compatible," but many DMs are skeptical about how the power scaling will work in practice.

### Transitioning Your Table

We recommend finishing up major campaign arcs before switching to the full 2024 rule set. However, many groups are already "house-ruling" in some of the more popular playtest features, such as the new Weapon Masteries.
