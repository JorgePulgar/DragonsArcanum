## The Expansion Dilemma

Every D&D player eventually looks beyond the *Player's Handbook*. The two most significant expansions are *Xanathar's Guide to Everything* (2017) and *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything* (2020). Both offer new subclasses, spells, and DM tools, but they serve different philosophies.

![Books Comparison](/images/tasha-vs-xanathar.jpg)

### Subclasses and Power

*Xanathar's* introduced fan-favorites like the Hexblade Warlock and Gloom Stalker Ranger, setting a new standard for design. *Tasha's* took it a step further, introducing the Artificer class and generally raising the power level of the game. If you want the strongest possible character, *Tasha's* is essential.

## DM Tools

This is where *Xanathar's* shines. Its section on "Downtime Activities," "Traps," and "Tool Proficiencies" provides practical, usable rules that fill the gaps in the DMG. *Tasha's* focuses more on high-concept tools like "Group Patrons" and "Magical Environments," which are cool but less universally useful.

### Customization

*Tasha's* changed the game with "Custom Lineage," allowing you to detitle racial stats from biology. This flexibility allows for almost any character concept to be mechanically viable. *Xanathar's* is more traditional in its approach.

## The Verdict

If you are a DM or a new player looking to expand your options without breaking the game, start with **Xanathar's Guide to Everything**. If you are a veteran player who wants to optimize builds or play an Artificer, grab **Tasha's Cauldron of Everything**.
