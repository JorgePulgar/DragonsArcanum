## The Battle of the Starter Sets

When it comes to starting D&D 5e, two names always come up: *Lost Mine of Phandelver* and *Dragon of Icespire Peak*. While both are set in the vicinity of the town of Phandalin, they offer vastly different experiences for players and DMs alike.

![Phandalin Map](/images/strahd-cover.jpg)

### Narrative vs. Modular Design

*Lost Mine of Phandelver* is a classic narrative adventure. It has a clear beginning, middle, and end, with a strong cast of NPCs that reappear throughout the story. In contrast, *Dragon of Icespire Peak* uses a "Job Board" system. Players pick modular quests from a board in town, making it feel more like a video game sandbox.

## Mechanics and Support

One area where *Icespire Peak* shines is in its introduction of "Sidekick" rules. These allow a single player and a DM to play a full campaign together, which is a fantastic addition for smaller groups. *Phandelver*, however, provides a more traditional party experience with its pre-generated characters.

### The Big Bad Evil Guys

The fight against the young white dragon, Cryovain, in *Icespire Peak* is undoubtedly more epic in scale than the final confrontation in *Phandelver*. However, the villain of *Phandelver*—The Black Spider—has much more personal motivation and presence throughout the campaign.

## The Verdict

If you want a cohesive story that feels like a classic fantasy epic, go with **Lost Mine of Phandelver**. If you prefer a loose sandbox where you can pick and choose missions based on your mood, **Dragon of Icespire Peak** is the better choice. Either way, you are in for a great time in the Forgotten Realms.
